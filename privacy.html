Nordly privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Nordly is a compass, level and outdoor-tools app for Android and Wear OS. It has no account, no analytics and no tracking of its own. This page lists everything that leaves your phone.

Weather

When weather is on, your coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo.com to get the forecast. Turn weather off in Settings → Weather & data and nothing is sent. Searching for a place sends the text you type to Open-Meteo's geocoding service.

Place names

To show your town's name, Nordly asks your phone's built-in place-name service (Android's Geocoder). On most phones that service is provided by Google.

Ads (free version)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK receives your device's advertising ID, IP address and how you interact with ads. In the EEA and UK you're asked for consent first, and you can change your answer at any time in Settings → About → Ad privacy choices. Nordly never passes your location to the ad service. See how Google uses data.

Nordly Plus

With Plus there are no ads and the ad SDK isn't started. Purchases are processed by Google Play; Nordly only learns whether your Google account owns Plus.

Wear OS

If you use the watch app, your phone shares weather, place, altitude and your chosen look with your own watch over the Wear OS connection. This doesn't pass through any Nordly server.

Stays on your phone

Compass and level readings, altitude, waypoints, pressure history and settings never leave your device. Camera images are shown live and are never saved or sent.

Permissions

Location, camera and notifications are optional. If you say no, the rest of Nordly keeps working.

Children

Nordly isn't directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue at github.com/S1nper/S1nper.github.io/issues.
